FAQ

How long does it take?

It depends on the size of the project. Once we see the drawings and brief, we give you an exact timeline in writing.

We only have drawings. Is that enough?

Yes. We build the 3D model ourselves from the architectural and floor-plan drawings.

Will the showroom work on our website?

Yes. Both as a separate link and embedded in your site.

Who updates prices and availability?

You do, through a simple spreadsheet. Changes appear in the showroom right away.

What is a 3D showroom?

A virtual sales office that opens in the browser. Buyers rotate the 3D building, pick a floor and apartment, see price and availability, and take a 360° tour inside.

Does it work on phones? Is an app needed?

No app needed. The showroom opens from one link in any phone, tablet or computer browser, and on the big screen in your sales office.

Do you only work in Tashkent?

We are based in Tashkent. The 3D model is built from drawings, so a project can start remotely too.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the size of the building, the number of apartment types and the services you need. We review your drawings and send an exact price and timeline in a written proposal.

Show your project before it's built.

Send us your drawings. Together we'll work out which service you need, the timeline and the price.
