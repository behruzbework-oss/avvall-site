Scale model or 3D showroom: what works better for developers?

For years the physical scale model was the main tool for selling off-plan homes. Today buyers increasingly choose an apartment on their phone. Here is a simple comparison.

AVVALL team · November 19, 2026

Send a requestOpen the demo showroom

Physical scale model: pros and cons

A scale model impresses in the sales office: buyers can see the building up close. But it takes weeks to make, it sits in one place and it cannot show the apartments inside. If the project changes, rebuilding the model is hard.

What a 3D showroom changes

  • Built once, opens on any phone
  • Shows not only the building but every apartment inside — with a 360° tour
  • Live prices and statuses: a sold apartment is marked instantly
  • Buyers in other cities or abroad choose without visiting the office
  • One link can go to Telegram, your website or an ad

At a glance

  • Where it is seen: model — in the sales office; showroom — anywhere
  • Apartment interior: model — no; showroom — 360° tour
  • Prices and status: model — a separate price list; showroom — on the card, always current
  • Changes: model — rebuild; showroom — in the admin panel

Bottom line

A scale model can remain a nice addition for the office. But when most buyers decide on their phone, a 3D showroom works better as the main sales tool.
