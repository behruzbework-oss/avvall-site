Interactive 3D showroom for property developers

The building is still going up, yet buyers already explore it in the browser: they pick a floor, open the layout and walk through the apartment in 360°.

Send a requestOpen the demo showroom

What is a 3D showroom

A 3D showroom is a virtual sales office that opens on your website or from a single link. It is built on an accurate 3D model of the building: facade, floors, apartment layouts and interiors. Buyers choose a home without visiting the office, and your sales team spends its time closing deals instead of explaining plans.

What buyers see

  • Rotate the building 360° and view it at different times of day
  • Click a floor to open its apartment layouts
  • Each apartment card shows area, price and status: available, reserved or sold
  • Take a room-by-room 360° tour inside the apartment
  • Open it on a phone, tablet, computer or the big screen in your sales office — no app needed

What developers get

  • Lower spending on physical scale models and show flats
  • Sales start at the foundation stage — buyers see the finished home today
  • Buyers in other cities and abroad can choose too
  • You update prices and availability yourself in the admin panel, and changes appear instantly

How we build it

  1. Brief: drawings, references and sales goals
  2. Accurate 3D model of the building and apartments
  3. Renders, interiors and 360° panoramas
  4. Showroom assembly and integration with your website
  5. Handover: you keep prices and statuses up to date

FAQ

Will the showroom work on our website?

Yes — both as a standalone link and embedded in your site.

Who updates prices and availability?

You do. In the admin panel you change each apartment’s price and status, and the showroom updates instantly.

We only have drawings. Is that enough?

Yes. We build the 3D model ourselves from architectural drawings and floor plans.

Show your project early.

Send us your drawings. We will agree on timing and price together.
