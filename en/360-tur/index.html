360° apartment tour for new developments

Buyers step into an apartment that does not exist yet: living room, kitchen, bedroom and bathroom — room by room.

Send a requestOpen the demo showroom

How it works

  • A 360° panorama in every room — turn the phone or drag with a finger
  • Move between rooms from a list or the apartment’s mini floor plan
  • One tour per apartment type; apartments of the same type share it
  • Opens with one tap from the apartment card in the 3D showroom

Why it helps sales

A floor plan drawing is abstract for most buyers. In a 360° tour they see room sizes, daylight from the windows and where the furniture goes. Fewer questions, and decisions get made without an office visit.

You control the panoramas

In the admin panel you replace panoramas, rename and reorder rooms, and link a tour to the right apartment type.

FAQ

Is an app needed?

No. The tour opens from a link in any browser.

Can the panoramas be replaced later?

Yes. Upload new panoramas at any time in the admin panel.

Does every apartment need its own tour?

No. Tours are made per apartment type and shared by apartments of that type.
